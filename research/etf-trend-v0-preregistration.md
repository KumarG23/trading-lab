# ETF trend challenger v0 — preregistration

Research only; no broker order path or capital/risk-policy changes. One fixed policy, no grid/search. Universe fixed at SPY, QQQ, IWM, EFA, EEM, TLT, GLD, VNQ (chosen as broad, historically established US-listed ETFs, **not** a point-in-time constituent universe). These tickers were selected with hindsight; delisted/closed funds are omitted. Do not claim survivorship-free results.

Data: Alpaca authenticated historical `1Day` stock bars, `adjustment=all` (splits and dividends), `feed=iex`, one symbol per request, all pages. Adjusted OHLC is a synthetic total-return proxy; dividend reinvestment and executable cash fills are not modeled literally. IEX-only OHLC can differ from consolidated prices, particularly opens/lows; no bid/ask, market impact or borrow modeling. Abort on missing/duplicate/bad bars or incomplete requested sessions. Exclude present/incomplete session. Date CLI boundaries are inclusive for `--start`, `--end`, `--dev-end`; use NYSE sessions and an explicit chronological holdout beginning at the session after dev-end. Require 200 prior completed closes of warmup before first scored day. Scores for each phase start at flat cash, with warmup from earlier data; split has no portfolio state carryover.

Policy: long-only, cash or up to two equally weighted ETFs; signal on prior *completed* close, rebalance only first NYSE session of each calendar week, execution next session open. Require SPY close > its 200-close SMA and candidate close > its 200-close SMA. Rank positive 63-session close/close momentum descending, ticker ascending tie break. No signals from execution-day OHLC. Mark daily equity at close. Sell exited positions and resize held ones at rebalance open, then buy selected names, equal target weight at the opening mark. Fractional shares and same-open execution are simplifying assumptions. No borrowing; cash may remain when no candidate. Daily standing trailing stop 12% below maximum **completed prior close since entry**, initially 12% below entry fill. At next open, gap below stop exits at open; otherwise low crossing exits at stop. Open exit slippage 10 bps adverse, entries 5 bps adverse, fee $0.005/share/side. After non-rebalance-day stops, proceeds remain cash until next weekly rebalance; on a weekly open, gap-stop proceeds may fund other eligible names at that same opening but never the stopped name. At each open, gap stops on positions carried overnight execute **before** weekly rebalance, and those names cannot reenter that day. After rebalance, intraday lows may trigger stops. If rebalance sells at open where no gap stop fired, that sell precedes that day's low. Report weekly selection and its prior-close provenance. Ignore adjusted-bar pre/postmarket microstructure. No tax, spread, interest, financing, or fractional-share availability modeling. Initial notional is arbitrary 1.0 for normalized returns; explicit fixed-per-share fee is scaled with a $10,000 research notional (not a recommended account size).

Development acceptance gate (before opening holdout): development net return > 0, beats flat cash (same criterion), at least 10 completed sell/stop transactions, and at least 252 scored NYSE sessions. No tuning after seeing result; if gate fails do not fetch or score holdout and report `not_scored_development_failed`. If it passes, fetch and score holdout exactly once without modifications and label provisional regardless of outcome. Report both SPY buy-and-hold adjusted close return as context and trade count, turnover, CAGR/drawdown; this is not a matched executable benchmark. One chronological split specified by CLI and recorded in artifact; intended first run `--start 2021-10-01 --dev-end 2023-12-29 --end 2025-12-31`. The original 2020 start was rejected before scoring: available IEX bars begin 2020-07-27. This later start is a data-coverage correction, not performance-driven tuning; the 420-calendar-day warmup begins 2020-08-07. Absence/sparsity fails closed, not imputed. One run is exploratory evidence, not a promotion gate or live trading authorization.

## First registered result — 2026-09-22

The initial real-data run used 6,840 adjusted IEX daily bars across eight ETFs and 855 validated NYSE sessions through 2023-12-29. The scored development period, 2021-10-01 through 2023-12-29 (565 sessions), returned **-7.47% after modeled costs**, annualized **-3.41%**, with **-19.43% max drawdown**, 84 sell transactions, and 0 stops. SPY adjusted-close change over the same dates was **+14.70%** (context only, not matched execution). The preregistered positive-return development gate failed. The 2024-2025 holdout was **not fetched or scored**. Preserve this negative result; do not tune this policy against it and call it an edge.
