# V3 next-session earnings shadow rule v1 — preregistered

Preregistered at **2026-09-30T23:02:51Z**, before implementation and before any lane outcome. Effective only after the first successful intake has persisted `activation.json`; the later of that timestamp and preregistration is the eligibility boundary. MU first observed 2026-09-30T20:24Z is historical context, never a prospective plan. No inferred SEC publication-time decisions. This is a separate read-only experiment; V3 v0 is unchanged. No edge or execution authority is claimed.

## Denominator and capture

One row per distinct verified frozen-universe SEC `sec-edgar-ex-99.1` earnings event with `first_seen_at == available_at`, first observed on an XNYS session at or after its actual close and before 20:00 Eastern, after activation. The next XNYS session is the only decision session; weekends/holidays are skipped by calendar. Run intake at least once before an event to establish activation and after the next open (ideally every minute 09:35–09:48 ET). Do not retrospectively manufacture a candidate if the collector missed the decision window. A timely intake at 09:35–09:37 ET obtains the five IEX 1-minute bars opening 09:30 through 09:34, each completed by decision time. All five must be continuous, correctly formed, IEX and present. If the just-completed bars have not appeared yet, retry within the fixed two-minute window; at its end persist an abstention for missing bars. Also abstain for late intake, invalid bars, disallowed stop width or insufficient sizing. Before 09:35 the event awaits a decision. Persist first final decision immutably, never revise after later bars arrive. The recorded receipt time is *after* the source request, not the start of that request. A source request failure aborts the intake (no activation on first failure); do not record a misleading no-bar abstention from a failed API request.

## Fixed plan / resolution

Long-only stop entry one cent above the five-bar high, stop one cent below five-bar low, target at 2R; stop width 0.2%–5% of entry. Integer shares min(floor($200 / entry), floor($2 / stop distance)); at least one share. Deadline 10 minutes after actual decision time, no later than 90 minutes before session close. Use the existing offline V3 outcome resolver for completed continuous IEX bars, adverse gap/slippage, fees, same-bar stop precedence, no-fill, pending and data-gap. Request outcomes only after session close + 15 minutes. IEX is not SIP; no quote is a fill. All plans are counterfactual, not an executable portfolio return. Abstentions remain in the denominator, no-fill contributes zero dollars, pending/gaps carry no P&L. Compare only against no-trade zero and always-admit fixed-rule baseline; no model verdict or broker authority. Any changed thresholds require a newly versioned rule before new observations.
